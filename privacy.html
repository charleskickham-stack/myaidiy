Privacy Policy

My AI DIY · Last updated 13 October 2026

In short: My AI DIY has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your jobs, photos and answers stay on your iPhone. The AI runs on your device using Apple Intelligence. We, the developer, never receive your data.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your repair jobs (what's wrong, your answers, photos you take or choose, the guide and your progress) are stored only on your iPhone.
  • Your settings (how handy you are, your first name and the helper you choose) are stored only on your iPhone.

AI, camera, microphone and photos

  • AI: working out what's wrong and writing guides uses Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. Your descriptions and photos are not sent to us or to any other AI company.
  • Camera and photos: used only when you choose to take or pick a photo or video, so the app can look at the problem or read an appliance's label. Text on labels is read on your device.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: used only while you tap "Talk", to turn what you say into text. This uses Apple's speech recognition, which may process audio on your device or, if needed, on Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy.

Asking family or a friend

  • Video call, phone and text message: these open Apple's FaceTime, Phone or Messages apps with the person you chose. Nothing is sent until you press Send or start the call yourself.
  • Live help: if you choose to share a job live, its details, your progress through the steps, and the messages and photos you exchange are stored in your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit and shared only with the people you invite. We cannot see them. Tapping "Stop" or deleting the job removes the shared copy.

Other things

  • Notifications are used for step timers and live help updates. You can turn them off in Settings.
  • "Look it up online" opens a web search in your browser. What you do there is covered by that website's own privacy policy.
  • Siri: voice commands such as "Next step in My AI DIY" are handled by Apple's Siri.
  • We don't sell or share data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.
  • The app isn't directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete a job from the job's page, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your iPhone. Shared live help data is removed when you stop sharing or delete the job.
